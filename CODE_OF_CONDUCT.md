# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ytmine to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age, background, identity, experience level or language ability. Many contributors are language learners, so questions and mistakes are welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Accept that people may disagree, and keep disagreements respectful.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or publishing someone else's private information.
- Sexualized language or attention that is unwelcome.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may ban people who do so repeatedly or seriously.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers through the email address on the repository owner's GitHub profile, or open a private security advisory on the repository. Reports will be handled privately and fairly.

## Scope

This code applies in the project's repositories, issue trackers and discussions, and wherever someone represents the project in public.
